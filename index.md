# JARVIS – Privacy Policy

JARVIS is a personal desktop assistant used only by its owner, Roman.
It accesses the owner's own Gmail to show and summarize his email on his own computer. 
Email content is processed locally on that computer and is not sold, shared, or sent to third parties.
Access tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager on the same computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
